02 / About

About ODL India

ODL India manufactures furniture for workplaces, classrooms, reception areas and other shared spaces. The range includes workstations and conference tables, along with seating, shelving and beds.

The catalogue also lists agricultural trailers and trailer-mounted tankers. For a bulk requirement, include quantities, intended use and any procurement specifications in your enquiry.
